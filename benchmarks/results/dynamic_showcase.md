These historical cycle times use the controller-reported internal timer. At this revision,
the dynamic controller stopped that timer before its final bookkeeping. Fresh runs of
`benchmarks/dynamic_showcase.jl` now measure wall time around the complete `solve!` call;
the historical values in this report have not been replaced. Timing is from a laptop whose
speed varies by about 2.5× between runs; it is not a real-time claim.

**In this session:**
- **Machine state.** The dynamic race was measured while the laptop was in its slower
  state, so its median (88–98 ms) is higher than that of the six-obstacle runs measured
  afterwards (62 ms). In an earlier fast state, the dynamic race had a 45 ms median.
- **Reproducibility.** Every repetition produced the same trajectory statistics (laps,
  iterations, clearances). The outcome does not depend on timing, because the showcases
  set no wall-clock budget.
